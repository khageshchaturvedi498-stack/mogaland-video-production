# Mogaland Academy: AI Avatar Video Production

AI avatar video production for Mogaland Academy, a Web3 financial literacy platform on the Open Campus EDU Chain. This repo documents the video work across two markets: India and Iraq.

**Author:** Khagesh Chaturvedi, Senior Advisor, India Strategy & Financial Partnerships, Mogaland Academy

## Output at a glance

| Market | Videos produced | Format | Presenters |
|---|---|---|---|
| India | About 50 | About 1 minute each, AI avatar | Professor Money and Professor Maya |
| Iraq | About 40 | AI avatar | Two newly created avatars (one male, one female) |

## Workflow

1. **Script:** Write a short, plain-language script for each video, with an opening, a core explanation, and a closing.
2. **Images:** Create presenter and background images with ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot.
3. **Avatar video:** Generate the presenter video in HeyGen from the script and the approved avatar.
4. **Editing:** Use Claude to tighten scripts, captions, and on-screen text when needed.
5. **Quality check:** Check accuracy, pacing, branding, and consistency before publishing.

## Tools

- **HeyGen:** AI avatar video generation
- **ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot:** Image generation for presenters and visuals
- **Claude:** Script writing and video editing support
